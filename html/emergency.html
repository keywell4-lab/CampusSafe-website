📞 Call 112
🔥

Fire Emergency

In case of fire, move away from danger, alert people nearby, and contact emergency services immediately.

📞 Call 112

STAY SAFE

What To Do During An Emergency

1️⃣

Stay Calm

Try to remain calm and quickly assess the situation before taking action.

2️⃣

Move To Safety

Move yourself and others away from immediate danger whenever it is safe to do so.

3️⃣

Call For Help

Contact campus security or the appropriate emergency service as soon as possible.

4️⃣

Give Clear Information

Explain what happened and provide your location so responders can find you quickly.
