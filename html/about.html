ABOUT CAMPUSSAFE

About CampusSafe

A digital platform designed to make campus safety reporting, emergency assistance, and lost and found management easier.

OUR PURPOSE

Making Campus Safer

CampusSafe provides students and members of the campus community with a simple way to communicate safety concerns and access important information.

🛡️

Safety First

We encourage students to speak up about incidents and safety concerns instead of ignoring situations that could put people at risk.

📢

Easy Reporting

CampusSafe makes it easier to record and submit information about incidents that happen within the campus community.

🤝

Community Support

A safer campus requires everyone to work together by sharing information and helping one another.

SIMPLE PROCESS

How CampusSafe Works

1️⃣

Identify The Situation

Recognize the safety concern, emergency, lost item, or found item that needs attention.

2️⃣

Choose A Service

Select incident reporting, Lost & Found, or Emergency Help depending on your situation.

3️⃣

Provide Information

Enter clear and accurate information so the situation can be understood properly.

4️⃣

Take Action

Follow the appropriate safety instructions and contact emergency services when necessary.

Your Safety Matters

Do not ignore a dangerous situation. Speak up, stay aware of your surroundings, and seek help whenever necessary.
